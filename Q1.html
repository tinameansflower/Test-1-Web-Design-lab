Inventor of the Web: Tim Berners Lee

Tim Berners Lee

Tim Berners-Lee was born on June 8, 1955, and is a well-known computer scientist, specifically for creating the World Wide Web, being the inventor of HTML, and the URL system. Additionally, he is the founder and director of the W3C (World Wide Web Consortium), responsible for the development and evolution of the web that he originally invented. He has contributed to the technical/digital industry as well as the academic industry, being a professorial researcher at the University of Oxford and a professor emeritus at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.
